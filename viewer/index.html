Getting started
  1. Look around: drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
  2. Walk: press Walk (bottom right), then WASD + mouse; Esc to leave.
  3. Measure: open the panel (□, top left) → Measure Height, Cross-Section, SLOPE, or click any building.
  4. Check accuracy: Upload reference DSM gives RMSE / MAE / correlation and an error map; Ground Control recalibrates the DSM from known heights.
  5. Take the data: Download DSM saves the GeoTIFF (absolute DSM, or rDSM for images without coordinates).
